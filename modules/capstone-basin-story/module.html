3 · Hydrological signatures & flow duration curve

  

    The Lyne–Hollick BFI (a filter output, not a measured groundwater fraction) and the
    Richards–Baker flashiness index (Baker et al., 2004) compress the whole record into
    two numbers. The Flow Duration Curve (FDC) shows what fraction of time any given discharge
    is exceeded — a steep FDC means flashy; a flat one means steady. Qx is the flow exceeded x% of the time.
    Run the cell, then compare the FDC shape to the client-side plot rendered below.
